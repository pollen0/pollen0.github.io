# About

I'm Paul Lin, based in New York.

This site holds three things:

- **Training notes** — deep technical write-ups on AI and machine learning, written to teach, not just to record. Everything here I can derive or build from scratch — that's the bar for publishing it.
- **Thoughts** — shorter weekly notes on whatever I'm chewing on.
- **Compute** — working notes on compute as a commodity and a tradable asset class: the players, the benchmarks, and why the market exists.

The messy middle — drafts, failed attempts, quiz answers — stays private. What you see here is what survived.

**A note on process:** all notes here are written by me first, then cleaned up and fact-checked with AI. The thinking is mine; the AI just tidies and verifies.
