# Itô

Itô (co-founders Affaan Mustafa and Alejandro Soumah) is a compute and inference cost trading platform. On October 12, 2026, the Itô team joined Jump Trading to run its compute trading business — leading Jump's newly created internal compute trading desk, which has already begun trading in the market.

Itô started out aiming to build infrastructure and contract markets to hedge risks in centralized AI supply chains that existing tools couldn't address. After speaking with 1,000+ funds, companies, VCs, and industry participants, the team zeroed in on the middle market for compute and inference costs.

The mid-market squeeze they found: capacity contracts typically run 3–5 years with ~30% paid upfront, while late-seed through Series C companies settle revenue monthly and by usage. Secondary-market prices for popular chips are highly volatile — and total contract values in these mid-market clusters run into the low-to-mid hundreds of millions.

The team brings backgrounds in Goldman Sachs credit basket trading, open-source engineering tools, and supercomputing cluster research — and picked Jump for its credit, risk management, trading experience, and hardware capabilities.
