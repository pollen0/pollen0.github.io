# SF Compute

SF Compute (founders Evan Conrad and Alex Gajewski) is a pure spot marketplace — a live central-limit orderbook where you buy GPU time and sublease what you don't use.

~$52M raised, including a $40M Series A at a $300M valuation (DCVC/Wing, November 2025). No derivatives, no index, no licenses.

Its strategic role, as I see it: being the price-discovery tape that everyone else's futures may eventually settle against.
